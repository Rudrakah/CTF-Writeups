Challenge

The challenge says:

I wrote you another song. Put the flag in the picoCTF{} flag format.

The challenge provides a song as the main clue. The goal is to identify the artist/band and place the answer inside the picoCTF flag format.

Approach

This is a simple song-identification challenge.

The song clue points to:

Bon Jovi

For the flag format, the space is removed and the artist name is written as:

BONJOVI

Step 1 – Identify the Artist

Analyze the provided song and identify the artist.

The answer is:

Bon Jovi

Step 2 – Convert to Flag Format

The challenge asks us to use the picoCTF flag format:

picoCTF{answer}

Using the identified artist:

picoCTF{BONJOVI}

Step 3 – Submit the Flag

Enter the following flag into the challenge submission field:

picoCTF{BONJOVI}

The challenge accepts it as the correct flag.

Flag

picoCTF{BONJOVI}

Tools Used

Song identification

picoCTF challenge interface

Key Learning

Not every CTF challenge requires technical exploitation.

General Skills challenges can involve identifying information from clues.

Always follow the requested flag format exactly.

Spaces or formatting in the answer may need to be removed when constructing the flag.

Final Solution
